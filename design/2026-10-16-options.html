Aggie Invitational: live scoring and results

Three directions for review. Nov 6–8, 2026, PEAP, College Station. One 60-arrow 18m round, five shooting lines (Fri 6pm; Sat 8am, 1pm, 6pm; Sun 9am), about 510 archers in about 50 divisions, top 3 per division awarded Sunday.

Sample scores below are real 2025 Aggie Invitational results pulled from EOS. Mix and match freely.

1 · Ringer sister2 · Line by line3 · Venue board + awards

In every option

  • Live scores from old EOS (api.eyesonscore.com), refreshed every 20 seconds, same calls as the Texas Ringer live board
  • Leaderboard for every division: 1st half, 2nd half, total, 10s, 9s (WA indoor tiebreak order)
  • Tap an archer to see their scorecard end by end
  • Phone first; works on the gym Wi-Fi with no app install
  • After the event, the page freezes to final results (no live calls)
  • Past results archive: 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025 are all in EOS and can be loaded
  • Static site on GitHub Pages, free, handles traffic spikes
  • Maroon and white, text wordmark only, no university logo unless the club gives us theirs
